# Command palette and shortcuts {#top}

## Command palette {#palette}

[[K]] opens a search over hosts, snippets, workspaces and actions. Prefixes narrow it: `@` hosts, `#` snippets, `>` actions, `?` this guide. Typing `user@host`, `host:port`, `telnet://host` or `serial://PORT` offers to connect directly, and the serial ports of this computer are listed as **Serial console** actions.
